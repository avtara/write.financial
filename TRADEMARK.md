## What you may do without asking

- **Self-host the unmodified software** for yourself, your family, your team, or your organization, and keep the name and logo the software ships with. Running an instance does not make it an official write.financial service, so do not describe it as one to other people. If you open an unmodified instance to the public, name the service after yourself (for example "Budget app by Example Co., powered by write.financial"), not "write.financial".
- **Refer to the project by name (nominative use)**: say that your fork, article, tutorial, package, integration, or hosting offer is "based on write.financial", "a fork of write.financial", or "compatible with write.financial", and link to this repository, as long as it is accurate and does not suggest endorsement.
- **Use the Marks to discuss or review** the project, in news, blog posts, talks, and teaching material.
- **Keep the name in the code**: package names, copyright notices, license headers, and attribution required by the AGPL must stay intact. This policy never requires you to remove them.

## What requires a rebrand

- **Modified public deployments.** If you run a modified version of write.financial for people outside your own household, team, or organization (whether free or paid), replace the name and logo in the user interface, emails, domains, and marketing with your own, and do not use a name or logo that is confusingly similar (for example "write.financial Pro" or "writefinancial.app").
- **Forks and redistributions** that are published under their own name, including app stores, container registries, and package registries, must use their own name and logo. You may say "based on write.financial" as described above.

## What you may not do

- Use the Marks in a way that suggests your product, service, fork, or company is official, endorsed, sponsored, or certified by write.financial or the project owner.
- Offer a hosted service called "write.financial" or a confusingly similar name, or use the Marks in a domain name, social media account, or business name.
- Use a modified version of the logo, or the logo as part of your own logo or product branding.
- Use the Marks in a misleading, defamatory, or unlawful way.
